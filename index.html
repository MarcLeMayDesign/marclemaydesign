Instructional Design and Learning Experience Specialist

Hi! I’m Marc LeMay, a designer with over two decades of experience crafting learning for adults and developing large-scale creative projects. I specialize in making complex subjects digestible and engaging, especially for reluctant learners. Music theory students and busy parents often come to me skeptical, but leave with new perspectives on the material.

Meet the Designer

My twin passions are education and creative collaboration.

As an Educator

  • 20years, 6 universities, 1,200+ students
  • 13courses built from scratch
  • 500+private students, tailor-made lessons for each of them
  • Alignment: all materials driven by learning objectives
  • Assessments test deep engagement, not just memorization: analytical writing and creative projects, rather than exams
  • Translating complex theoretical material into digestible, engaging learning, especially for learners who come to the material skeptical
  • Strong public-facing communication skills, which carry into instructional design, learning facilitation, and proficiency in communicating with LLMs
  • Appreciation for gray areas: real knowledge production often involves addressing multiple conflicting ideas at once
  • Accessibility and LMS management: eight courses moved fully online during the pandemic, plus five more taught as hybrids; recorded and captioned video lectures, tracked engagement analytics, and authored quizzes and discussion boards in Canvas and D2L Brightspace

As a Creator

  • 25years as a composer, arranger, songwriter, and sound designer
  • 33contemporary classical pieces composed, many on commission, across a variety of instrumentations
  • 30plays (music and/or sound design), 3 film scores, 2 albums (orchestral arrangements)
  • Constant adaptation to new technology: engraving software, digital audio workstations, synthesizers, and much more
