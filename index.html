Instructional design & learning experience

Twenty years teaching people who didn’t want to be there.

Hi — I’m Marc LeMay. I taught music theory to students who were required to take it, and it taught me what I design for now: you have to earn someone’s attention before you can teach them anything. So I cut the exams and assigned projects instead — make something, then defend why it works. Based in Tallahassee, working remote.
